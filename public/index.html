ℹ️ About the Addon
Chole Bhature is a high-performance stream meta-sorter designed to drastically improve your viewing experience.

It dynamically curates, cleans, and prioritizes links from multiple sources into a single optimized feed, removing dead, slow, or duplicate streams automatically.
